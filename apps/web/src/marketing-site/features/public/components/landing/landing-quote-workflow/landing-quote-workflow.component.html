Built-in quote workflow

Send quote requests to conveyancers - all inside the platform

Once your report is ready, ConveyMate can automatically send a structured quote request to up to three conveyancers in your state. Your report summary, contract documents, and buyer questions are attached. Responses come back into a single comparison view - without you leaving the platform or sending emails manually.

  1. 01
    Define the request

    Choose the service, edit the buyer questions pre-filled from your report, and write an optional email introduction.

  2. 02
    Select a shortlist

    Browse or search the ConveyMate directory, filtered to your state. Pick up to three firms to receive the request.

  3. 03
    ConveyMate sends it automatically

    Each firm gets a secure quote link with the brief, your questions, and optional document access. Replies arrive in your dashboard comparison view.
