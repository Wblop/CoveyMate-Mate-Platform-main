What to upload for the best results

Upload complete PDF copies of your property pack. {{ uploadLimitSummary }} The more complete and readable the documents are, the more accurate the report will be.

Priority documents (PDF)

  • Contract of sale (full version, including particulars pages)
  • Special conditions, annexures, schedules, and signed variations
  • Title search and plan documents (easements, caveats, encumbrances)
  • Rates, water, and outgoings notices
  • Building and pest reports (if available)
  • For units/apartments: strata report, levy schedule, minutes, and by-laws

Quality rules that improve accuracy

  • Use digital text PDFs where possible (not photos of pages).
  • If scans are unavoidable, use high contrast, straight pages, and at least 300 DPI.
  • Upload full documents with all pages; do not crop margins, schedules, or signatures.
  • Keep one logical document per file and use clear file names.
  • Avoid password-protected or locked PDFs.

ConveyMate extracts text, builds embeddings, and retrieves evidence by semantic similarity. Cleaner, complete PDFs improve retrieval quality, reduce missing fields, and lower confusing output.
